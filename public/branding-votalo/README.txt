VOTALO — BRAND ASSETS PACK
==========================

The logo is the "living ring": three arcs (orange, violet, green) around a dot.
It is the same shape as the live results ring inside the app. The arcs are the
options of a vote, the dot is you: one person, one vote.

WHICH FILE TO USE
-----------------
- Website, docs, anything that can show vector:      svg/  (scales to any size)
- Light background (cream/white):                    *-light*  files
- Dark background (ink/black):                       *-dark*   files
- Social media profile picture:                      png/avatar-800.png  (or avatar-1600.png)
- Link preview / Open Graph / post header:           png/social-banner-1200x630-es.png  (or -en)
- Browser tab / app icon:                            svg/favicon.svg, png/favicon-16/32/48/180/512.png
- Logo with the name, for slides and documents:      svg/lockup-*.svg, png/lockup-*-2000.png
- One-color printing, stamps, embroidery:            svg/logo-mono-dark.svg (on light), logo-mono-light.svg (on dark)
- Hackathon or form "upload your logo" (square):     png/logo-primary-light-1024.png (transparent)

NAMING
------
logo-<variant>-<background>-<size>.<ext>
variant: primary | mono       background: light (for light backgrounds) | dark (for dark backgrounds)

COLORS
------
On light backgrounds          On dark backgrounds
Orange  #E07A00               #FFB454
Violet  #3B2BAE               #7C6CF0
Green   #14A877               #3DD9A4
Dot/text #16131C              #F4F1EA
Backgrounds: ink #0D0B12, cream #F7F4EE. Brand accent for buttons: #FF9F1C with dark text #1A1206.
The three arcs are separated by real gaps, so the logo still reads in one color and in grayscale.

TYPE
----
Wordmark: Bricolage Grotesque ExtraBold, outlined to paths in the SVGs (no font needed to use them).
Bricolage Grotesque and Geist are Google Fonts under the SIL Open Font License (free for commercial use).

CLEAR SPACE AND MINIMUM SIZE
----------------------------
- Clear space on every side: at least the diameter of the center dot multiplied by 2.
- Minimum size: icon 16 px on screen (use favicon.svg below 32 px), 6 mm in print.
  Lockup: 80 px wide on screen, 20 mm in print.

DON'T
-----
- Don't stretch or squash it. Scale proportionally.
- Don't change the arc colors or their order (orange, violet, green clockwise from the top).
- Don't rotate it, add shadows, bevels, glows or outlines.
- Don't close the gaps between the arcs or turn the ring into a full circle.
- Don't place it on a busy photo without a solid or darkened area behind it.
